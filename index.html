Perfect Euler Brick & Elliptic Curves

            
An Euler Brick requires integer edges and face diagonals: a²+b²=d₁², b²+c²=d₂², c²+a²=d₃². If the space diagonal g=√(a²+b²+c²) is also an integer, it becomes a Perfect Cuboid. Spohn & Bremner proved this corresponds to rational points on specific families of elliptic curves y² = x(x-α)(x-β). No perfect cuboid has been discovered up to edges exceeding 10¹².
